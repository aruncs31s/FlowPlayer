Privacy Policy for Music Sync

Last Updated: September 19, 2026

Music Sync ("we", "our", or "the App") is a peer-to-peer and local network companion application designed to synchronize, stream, and manage audio files between your Android device and personal computer. We are strictly committed to user privacy.

1. Zero Data Collection & Local Network Privacy

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or monetize any personal data, analytics, device identifiers, or user information. All network communication operates entirely within your private Local Area Network (Wi-Fi) directly with your desktop computer. No files or metadata are ever uploaded to cloud servers or third parties.

2. Permissions and Usage

Permission Usage
READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Accesses music files on your device to display them and allow streaming/syncing to your desktop.
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Saves incoming audio files synced from your desktop to your device's Music folder.
INTERNET & ACCESS_WIFI_STATE Runs an embedded local HTTP server for local Wi-Fi communication with the desktop app.
CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE Enables automatic peer discovery across your local Wi-Fi network.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC Maintains background file transfers and audio streaming without interruption by battery savers.

3. Advertisements & Analytics

The App contains no third-party advertisements, tracking libraries, or analytics SDKs.

4. Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any personal information from any user, including children under 13.

5. Contact

For questions regarding this policy, please reach out through the project's official support channels.
